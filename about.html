The Stylist

Sue Maistry

Background

Two Decades of Craft

Sue Maistry established her career as a Senior Stylist at Carlton Hair, where she mastered the technical standards of precision cutting and professional colour.

Today, she brings that senior-level expertise to her private studio in Fish Hoek, offering a one-on-one environment focused on technical excellence and client care.

For clients in the South Peninsula, this means access to a senior-level technical standard in a calm, professional environment.
